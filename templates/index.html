SkyArt Studio — User Guide & Manual
v2.0
Everything you need to know about designing, editing, and flying GPS Sky Art.

🚀 Quick Start Guide

Beginner Friendly

SkyArt turns drawings, shapes, and images into realistic, flyable GPS flight plans you can fly in real life with your airplane's GPS or autopilot!

1
Pick or Draw Art

Select a built-in shape (like the RV-7 Airplane or Heart), draw freehand with your mouse/touchscreen, or upload a photo.

2
Position & Scale

Drag the orange diamond anchor pin to place the art over your favorite flying area. Use the sliders to adjust size and rotation.

3
Export & Fly

Click Open in ForeFlight, scan the QR Code with your iPad, or download a Garmin FPL file for your cockpit navigator!

Pro Tip: By default, SkyArt automatically connects your departure and arrival airport to the art with clean transit legs, so you have a complete runway-to-runway flight plan!

🎨 Creating & Importing Artwork

You have multiple ways to create your flight art: pre-made aviation shapes, freehand drawing directly on the map, or uploading pictures.

Built-in Shape Presets

Click the Preset Shape dropdown in the sidebar to load instantly:

✈️ Airplane (RV-7) ❤️ Heart ⭐ Star 🍀 Four-Leaf Clover 💎 Diamond ⭕ Smooth Circle 🔤 Text "FLY"
Freehand Drawing Mode

Want to draw your own custom shape?

  1. Click the Freehand Draw button in the sidebar.
  2. Click and drag with your mouse, finger, or stylus directly over the map canvas.
  3. When you release, SkyArt automatically smooths the line, detects if you drew a closed loop, and creates optimized GPS waypoints!
Image Import & Vectorization (PNG, JPG, SVG, iPhone HEIC)

Click Import Image / Photo to trace any drawing, logo, cartoon, or sketch into GPS waypoints. SkyArt provides 3 specialized tracing modes:

Outline Contour
Traces only the outer perimeter silhouette of your image (like a cookie cutter). Ideal for logos, animals, and solid shapes.
Single Stroke Path (Recommended for Line Art & Sketches)
Built specifically for line drawings, coloring book pages, and black & white sketches! Instead of drawing double lines along the inside and outside of thick strokes, it extracts the center skeleton into one continuous, single-stroke flight path.
All Vectors
Captures all internal and external line segments for complex multi-stroke graphics.
Tracing Lightbox Layer (Trace by Hand)
Want to trace the art yourself? Projects your imported image as a semi-transparent layer directly on top of the aeronautical map. Automatically launches Freehand Draw mode with live opacity controls (10%–100%), color inversion, and scale alignment so you can sketch your own custom flight path with your mouse or stylus!
Detail Slider: Adjust how fine or simple the line tracing is.
Invert Colors: Check this if your image has white lines on a dark background.

📍 Map & Waypoint Tools

SkyArt gives you full interactive control over every vertex, leg, and dimension of your flight route directly on the aeronautical map.

🔶
Orange Diamond Center Pin

Click and drag the orange diamond in the middle of your artwork to move the entire drawing anywhere in the world.

↔️
Scale (Width & Height in NM)

Resize your art in Nautical Miles. Click the chain icon to lock or unlock aspect ratio for custom stretching.

🔄
Rotate (0° – 360°)

Spin the shape to align with favorable winds, mountain valleys, or avoid restricted airspace.

🔀
Reverse Route Direction

Toggle between flying your route clockwise or counter-clockwise with a single click.

Interactive Waypoint Editing
  • Drag to Move: Click and drag any numbered circle marker to fine-tune the waypoint position. On closed loop shapes, the start and finish point stay automatically linked.
  • Insert Waypoints: Click any + handle located halfway along any leg, or click anywhere directly on the blue route line to drop a new waypoint.
  • Right-Click to Delete: Right-click on any numbered waypoint marker to immediately delete it.
  • Hover Tooltips: Hover over any waypoint marker to see its exact coordinates, incoming & outgoing leg lengths (NM), compass tracks (°), turn angles, and autopilot cutoff distance. Hover over the route line to see leg distances and tracks.
Garmin & ForeFlight 99-Waypoint Ceiling: Most aviation GPS avionics limit user flight plans to 99 waypoints. SkyArt automatically enforces this limit so your exported flight plans load cleanly without truncation errors!

✈️ Autopilot & Loop-Backs

Patent-Grade Feature

Real aircraft autopilots cannot make instantaneous square or hairpin turns—they roll into turns early ("fly-by lead turns"). SkyArt provides specialized tools to preview and solve this.

Autopilot Ghost Track (Orange Dashed Line)

The orange dashed line previews the exact curved path your autopilot will fly at your chosen speed and bank angle.

  • Turn Radius Slider: Adjust the turn radius from 0.2 NM (slow ultralight/drone) up to 2.5 NM (high-speed piston/turboprop). A typical general aviation aircraft cruising at 120–140 knots flies with a ~1.0 NM turn radius.
  • Leg Clamping Detection: If a leg is shorter than the distance needed for a full turn, SkyArt automatically clamps the radius to fit the leg, clearly noting [clamped] in the tooltip so you know exactly what to expect.
Exterior Corner Loop-Backs (Keep Sharp Corners Crisp!)

When flying a star, airplane wingtip, or sharp geometric corner, a standard autopilot will cut the corner, rounding off your artwork and ruining the drawing.

How Loop-Backs Solve This: When you enable Corner Loop-Backs, SkyArt automatically inserts an exterior teardrop loop for every turn sharper than 60°. Your aircraft flies outside the shape, completes a smooth standard-rate 270° turn, and passes directly through the corner point! On flight tracking websites like FlightAware and FlightRadar24, your corners remain 100% sharp and true to the drawing!

Individual Corner Toggle: You don't have to enable loops everywhere. Click directly on any corner marker on the map to turn loop-backs on or off for that individual corner!

🛫 Airports & Transits

Connect your flight art directly to your departure and arrival airfields for a complete, seamless runway-to-runway flight plan.

Airport Search

Type any 3-letter or 4-letter FAA/ICAO code (e.g. E16, KPAO, KOSH), airport name, or city into the search box to set departure and arrival airfields.

Persistent Home Base

Click the Home Airport badge in the top bar to save your home base. Every time you open SkyArt, your home airport is automatically loaded!

Smart Entry & Exit Routing

When you check Connect Departure / Arrival:

  • Dashed green lines connect your departure runway to the start of the art, and your arrival airport to the finish.
  • No Slicing Through Art: SkyArt analyzes all vertices and automatically starts your art at the vertex closest to your departure airport so the transit leg never cuts awkwardly through the middle of your drawing!

Aircraft Performance Settings

Under the Aircraft Performance section in the sidebar, customize these two numbers to match your plane:

  • Cruise Speed (KTAS): Your true airspeed in knots (e.g. 110 kt for a C172, 160 kt for an RV-7, 180 kt for a Bonanza).
  • Fuel Burn (GPH): Your average cruise fuel flow in gallons per hour (e.g. 8.5 GPH for a Lycoming O-320, 10.5 GPH for an IO-360).

📲 ForeFlight & GPS Export

Export your sky art into your cockpit electronic flight bag (EFB) or panel GPS in seconds.

1. Open Directly in ForeFlight
If you are on an iPad, iPhone, or Mac with ForeFlight installed, tap Open in ForeFlight. ForeFlight opens immediately with the complete flight route, waypoints, and airport connections loaded in your FPL box!
2. ForeFlight QR Code (Wireless Import)
Designing on your desktop computer or laptop? Click the QR Code button, then open the camera app on your iPad or iPhone and scan the screen. A ForeFlight banner pops up on your iPad—tap it to instantly load the flight plan wirelessly!
3. Garmin / ForeFlight Flight Plan (.fpl)
Download the official aviation XML .fpl file. AirDrop or email it to your tablet to open in ForeFlight or Garmin Pilot, or copy it onto an SD card to load into your Garmin GTN 650/750, G3X Touch, or G1000!
4. GPX Route (.gpx)
Standard GPS exchange format compatible with handheld aviation navigators (Aera series), Dynon SkyView, FlyQ, CloudAhoy, and consumer GPS devices.
5. Google Earth 3D Path (.kml)
Visualizes your flight path in 3D in Google Earth or Google Maps to preview terrain clearance and scenic views.
6. Copy Waypoint String
Copies the clean coordinate string to your clipboard for quick copy-pasting into 1800wxbrief / Leidos for filing your VFR flight plan.

💡 Pro Pilot Tips & Best Practices

Advice from experienced flight art pilots to ensure a safe, fun, and picture-perfect flight.

Check Airspace & TFRs

Before takeoff, always check ForeFlight or SkyVector for local Class B/C/D airspace, Military Operations Areas (MOAs), and Temporary Flight Restrictions (TFRs). Position your artwork in open Class G or Class E airspace.

Smooth Air is Key

Fly early in the morning or late in the afternoon when thermal convection and turbulence are at their lowest. Smooth air allows the autopilot to fly straight, laser-sharp lines.

Use VFR Flight Following

Request VFR Flight Following from ATC. When checking in, you can politely advise: "We're maneuvering in this block for GPS navigation calibration / flight art." Controllers appreciate knowing your planned intentions!

Autopilot GPS Steering (GPSS)

Coupling your autopilot to GPS Navigation (NAV / GPSS mode) will automatically crab the aircraft for crosswinds, ensuring your recorded GPS track over the ground matches the artwork perfectly!
